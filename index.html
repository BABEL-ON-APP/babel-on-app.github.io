Intelligent dictation. Locally hosted. No subscription.

Local, open-source dictation for the Mac. Your voice never leaves your machine.

MIT license / Apple silicon / macOS 15 or later

01

You speak. The app cleans.

Press one key and speak. Press the key again to stop. The text goes in at your cursor, in any app.

Demo. The spoken words are: “um so can you uh move the call to three no wait four thirty on thursday”. The app removes “um”, “so”, “uh” and the self-correction “three no wait”. The text that goes in is: “Can you move the call to 4:30 on Thursday?”

New message

Can you move the call to 4:30 on Thursday?

Done
  • The app removes “um”, “uh” and stutters.
  • The app removes self-corrections.
  • Say “comma” or “new line” and the app writes the mark.
  • Numbers, times and emails get their written form.
  • The app adds capitals and punctuation.
02

One voice, the right shape.

The app shapes the text for the app you speak into.

AI chat

Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT

Can you check the notes and tell me what to change?

A clean prompt.

Terminal

Any terminal app

Always one line. No line break, so no command runs by mistake.

03

Private by design.

Runs on your Mac.

Speech recognition and text cleanup run on the Mac. Your voice and your words never leave it.

No account. No limits.

No plan and no telemetry. The only network use is one model download from Hugging Face, and you start it.

Open source, MIT.

Free, under the MIT license. Built from open models.

06

Get it.

Build the app from source. There is no binary download.

System needs

  • A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later)
  • macOS 15 Sequoia or later
  • About 1.2 GB of disk space for the two models
Build steps 
git clone https://github.com/BABEL-ON-APP/babel-on.git
cd babel-on
tools/fetch-llama.sh
tools/bundle.sh
open "build/BABEL ON.app"

The app guides you through the model download and the permissions on its first run.
