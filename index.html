01

You speak. The app cleans.

Press one key and speak. Press the key again to stop. The text goes in at your cursor, in any app.

Demo. The spoken words are: “um so can you uh move the call to three no wait four thirty on thursday”. The app removes “um”, “so”, “uh” and the self-correction “three no wait”. The text that goes in is: “Can you move the call to 4:30 on Thursday?”

New message

Can you move the call to 4:30 on Thursday?

Done
  • The app removes “um”, “uh” and stutters.
  • The app removes self-corrections.
  • Say “comma” or “new line” and the app writes the mark.
  • Numbers, times and emails get their written form.
  • The app adds capitals and punctuation.

03

Speak your language.

A second key dictates and translates. You speak in one language. The app types clean text in English, or in the language you choose. It runs on your Mac.

Spanish → English

eh pues la la reunión es mañana a las tres

The meeting is tomorrow at 3:00.

French → English

euh ben on on se retrouve à la gare à midi

Let’s meet at the station at noon.

German → English

ähm also ich ich schicke dir den Bericht heute Abend

I’ll send you the report tonight.

Italian → French

ehm allora ci ci vediamo domani alle nove

On se voit demain à neuf heures.
